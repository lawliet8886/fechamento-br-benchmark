What this follow-up can and cannot establish

The cases were designed after seeing the original findings. They are a targeted follow-up, not a blind holdout or a random sample of administrative work. Values and wording changed together, so a different answer does not identify its cause. Two repetitions are not evidence of general model reliability or statistical significance. SDK run caching was disabled; provider-side caching and effective seed support were not controlled.

All 72 prompts and answers were regraded and reconciled with six native parent runs and 72 distinct native conversations. This is a consistency check, not a signed third-party attestation. Codex provided a separate AI review, not a human or blind audit.

Original study and follow-up remain separate. Fictional records only. No inference, account, tracking or paid service is used by this page.
